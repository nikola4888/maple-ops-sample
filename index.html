1What this is

This is a sample community-operations plan for Maple Finance, written from the outside using only Maple's public site, docs, blog and the channels Maple itself lists. Maple is the object of the exercise; the method, how I split audiences, route questions, escalate and review, is mine and would carry over to any protocol. I have no access to Maple's team, tools or data, and nothing here describes how Maple actually runs its community. I claim no results, because I have not done this work for Maple.

Shorter contrasts, same method: Centrifuge, whose public story has moved on from its first framing, and Plume, where the product surface is wide but the routing between channels is thin.
