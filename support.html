Outside support sample: Maple Finance

Based only on public materials, checked 7 and 8 October 2026. Not affiliated with Maple.

Written by Nikola

One help article, one troubleshooting tree, one escalation card. Every answer ends in a Maple page or a Maple door. Nothing here is advice.

1I deposited and I cannot get the money out.

Withdrawing from syrupUSDC or syrupUSDT is a request, not an instant transfer. You start it from the Portfolio page in the Maple app. Your request joins a queue and is processed first-in, first-out as liquidity becomes available. While it waits, your position keeps earning. When it is processed, the funds go straight to your wallet with no second transaction from you.[2]

The normal case

Maple describes the normal case in two slightly different ways. The lender FAQ says liquidity is normally instant and that in rare cases a withdrawal can take around 24 hours.[1] The Withdrawals page says most withdrawals are processed in under 24 hours.[2]

The documented long tail

Both pages give the same maximum: up to 30 days.[1][2] That is the documented ceiling, not a delivery estimate. The FAQ says the app shows how much liquidity is available to withdraw.[1] The Withdrawals page also describes swapping syrupUSDC on a DEX instead of waiting; it notes that fees, slippage and risk are on you, and that the swapped amount stops earning.[2] That is Maple's description of an option, not a recommendation from me.

The Withdrawals page names syrupUSDC and syrupUSDT. For syrupUSDG, the lender FAQ is the page that covers all three.[1][2]

If your own request looks stuck

Open Intercom chat inside the Maple web app. Maple's docs list it as the place for troubleshooting and support.[3] Don't post your wallet address or transaction details in public chat, and don't accept help from anyone who DMs you first.

The sentence support must not say

"It'll be back in your wallet within 24 hours." The docs describe what usually happens and the maximum. Neither is a promise, and a moderator's promise is the one users remember.

2Troubleshooting tree

Five branches. Each one ends in a Maple link or a Maple door. Nothing on this page is a judgement call about someone's own situation.

  1. "Can I use this from my country?"

    Link. Available Jurisdictions, which lists where syrupUSDC, syrupUSDT and syrupUSDG are not available.[4]

    Not from support. A yes or no for any individual person.

  2. "The name I know isn't the name on the site."

    Link. For Syrup versus Maple: the New Wallets FAQ.[5] For the pool names in the institutional guide: the Institutional Secured Lending lender guide.[6] For Maple Earn, Maple Credit and Maple Embed: the 30 September brand post.[7]

    Door. If the question is about your own position under an old name: Intercom in the web app.[3]

  3. "I still hold MPL."

    Link. SYRUP FAQs. The docs state that MPL to SYRUP conversion ended on 30 April 2025 and that MPL no longer has governance or staking utility.[8]

    Not from support. Hints that a new window might open.

  4. "Do I need to migrate for CCIP 2.0?"

    Link. Maple's 28 September post: holders and integrators need take no action, token addresses stay the same, and there is nothing to claim, migrate or approve.[9] New pool addresses are published in the crosschain docs.[10]

  5. "Someone DM'd me to migrate, claim or get support."

    Link. The same 28 September post: there is nothing to claim, migrate or approve.[9] Get in touch: Maple's listed support route is Intercom inside the web app.[3]

    Door. Report the account in the community Telegram group that Maple lists on Get in touch.[3]

3Escalation card

Same rules as the main plan, short enough to pin.

  1. Answer from docs

    Anyone on the team, with a link every time

    • How withdrawals work and the documented maximum
    • Restricted jurisdictions
    • MPL conversion is closed
    • CCIP 2.0 needs no holder action
    • Where Intercom is

  2. Raise it

    Handed to the team through the channel they name

    • A specific request that looks stuck (move it to Intercom)
    • Impersonators and fake migration links
    • Docs that contradict each other

  3. Never promise

    Not by anyone, in any tone

    • Yield
    • Eligibility
    • Credit risk
    • Timing
